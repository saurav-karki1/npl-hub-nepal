## Page Structure

### Home

`/`

Main dashboard containing:

* NPL season overview
* Next match
* Today's matches
* Upcoming matches
* Recent results
* Points table preview
* Latest news
* Top performers
* Teams

### Matches

`/matches`

* Today's matches
* Upcoming matches
* Completed matches

### Match Details

`/matches/[match-id]`

* Teams
* Date and time
* Venue
* Match status
* Score
* Result
* Player performances

### Schedule

`/schedule`

Complete tournament schedule.

### Points Table

`/points-table`

* Team
* Matches
* Wins
* Losses
* Points
* Net Run Rate

### Teams

`/teams`

List of NPL teams.

### Team Details

`/teams/[team-id]`

* Team information
* Squad
* Matches
* Results
* Statistics

### Players

`/players`

List of players.

### Player Profile

`/players/[player-id]`

* Player information
* Team
* Batting statistics
* Bowling statistics
* Tournament statistics

### Stats

`/stats`

* Batting statistics
* Bowling statistics
* Team statistics
* Top performers

### News

`/news`

List of NPL news and updates.

### News Article

`/news/[article-slug]`

Individual news article.

### Results

`/results`

Completed match results.

### Tickets

`/tickets`

Verified ticket information and purchasing information when available.

### Where to Watch

`/where-to-watch`

Verified broadcasting and streaming information.

### About

`/about`

Information about NPL Hub Nepal and its independent status.
